Privacy Policy for ShrinkiFly

Last Updated: November 4, 2026

ShrinkiFly ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our image compression service.

1. Image and File Privacy

We do not upload, store, or access your images. All image processing happens entirely within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your files never leave your device. We cannot see, access, or retain any images you process through ShrinkiFly. This ensures maximum privacy and security for your sensitive photos and documents.

2. Information We Collect

While we don't collect your images, we do collect certain information automatically through third-party services:

  • Device Information: Browser type, version, operating system, and screen size
  • IP Address: Your approximate geographic location (city/region level)
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on site, and referral source
  • License Information: If you purchase a Pro license, your license key is stored locally on your device

3. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Google AdSense: Displays personalized advertisements based on your interests. Google uses cookies to track your browsing across websites to show relevant ads.
  • Google Analytics: Helps us understand how visitors use our site to improve user experience. We track page views, session duration, and user flow.
  • LocalStorage: Saves your Pro license key (if activated) locally on your device for convenience. You can clear this data anytime through your browser settings.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete existing ones. However, disabling cookies may affect site functionality. You can also opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google AdSense: For displaying advertisements. Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics: For website analytics and performance monitoring. Privacy Policy
  • LemonSqueezy: For processing Pro license purchases. Privacy Policy

5. Your Privacy Rights

Under GDPR (European Union) and CCPA (California), you have the following rights:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to request deletion of your data
  • Right to opt-out of data sales (note: we don't sell data)
  • Right to opt-out of personalized advertising
  • Right to data portability

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@shrinkifly.pics

6. Data Security

Since we do not upload or store your images on our servers, your image data remains secure on your device. Any data we collect through Google Analytics is handled according to Google's security standards and is used solely for improving our service.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:
