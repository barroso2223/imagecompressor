About ShrinkiFly

ShrinkiFly is a powerful and easy-to-use online tool designed to compress your images without compromising quality. Whether you're a web developer looking to speed up your website, a blogger wanting to optimize post images, or just someone trying to send smaller files via email, ShrinkiFly has you covered.

Our tool processes everything right in your browser, meaning your images are never uploaded to a server. This ensures your privacy and provides near-instant compression. We support popular formats like JPEG, PNG, and the modern WebP format, giving you full control over the output.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is ShrinkiFly free to use?

Yes! The core functionality of ShrinkiFly is completely free for single-image compression. For users who need to process multiple images at once, we offer a Pro version that unlocks batch processing, drag-and-drop uploads, and a "Download All as ZIP" feature for maximum convenience.

Are my images secure?

Absolutely. Your privacy is our top priority. All image compression happens directly on your computer within your web browser. Your files are never uploaded to our servers or seen by anyone else.

What image formats do you support?

You can upload most standard image formats. For output, you can choose between JPEG, PNG, and WebP. For JPEG and WebP formats, you can also adjust the compression quality to find the perfect balance between file size and image clarity.

How do I upgrade to Pro?

You can upgrade to Pro by purchasing a license key. Once you have your key, simply enter it into the "Have a Pro license?" field on the tool and click "Unlock Pro." Your browser will remember your license for future visits.
